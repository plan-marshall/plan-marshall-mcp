== Tasks

The specification sections named are the single statement of what each task builds.

. *The recovery built with its enrolment steps*: the daemon builds its per-project recovery with `ProjectRecovery.withEnrolment` of `pm-runtime` over the enrolment store, so that the enrolment check runs as step 1 and the owed enrolment audit records as step 2: link:../../../pm-mcp-core/doc/specification/runtime-lifecycle/01-startup-recovery-drain.adoc#per-project-recovery[Runtime Lifecycle § Per-Project Recovery], steps 1 and 2; link:../../../pm-mcp-core/doc/specification/credentials-and-enrolment/02-enrolment-and-project-lifecycle.adoc#enrolment-integrity[Credentials & Enrolment § Enrolment Integrity], _Verification Points_.
. *The background pass over unenrolled projects*: the owed audit records of a project no longer enrolled, handed over by the background pass: § Per-Project Recovery, step 2.
. *Started at Ready*: the recovery pass started over all enrolled projects when the daemon is ready, and a call addressing a project waiting for its recovery: link:../../../pm-mcp-core/doc/specification/runtime-lifecycle/01-startup-recovery-drain.adoc#_startup_sequence[Runtime Lifecycle § Startup Sequence], _Ready_; § Per-Project Recovery, the opening paragraph.

Left out: the other recovery steps and the sweep (`lifecycle-wiring`); the jobs step (`job-runtime-wiring`).

== Done When

* Watch: none anchored to steps 1 and 2; the trace matrix names any it finds.
* Tests: a `*IT` of the packaged daemon per enrolment failure (a tampered sidecar, a widened mode, an invalid file, a duplicate `project_root`), each with its audit record at the next start and the project not served; an owed `.audit-intent` handed over at the next start, and one of an unenrolled project by the background pass; a call addressing a recovering project answered `runtime_recovering` on expiry of its wait.
