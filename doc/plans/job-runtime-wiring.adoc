== Tasks

* CDI producers of the job runner, the launch and the supervisor of `pm-runtime`.
* The job-token registry over the job records, the per-request binding of token, generation and job, the tool-level answer to a revoked token, and the tool set per role class.

== Done When

A worker connection sees exactly the tools of its role class; a call with a token of an ended job is refused `job_ended`; a call for an entity not leased to the generation is `job_scope_mismatch`.
