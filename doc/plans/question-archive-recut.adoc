== What

`cuioss/plan-marshall-telemetry` holds verification items taken from real operator questions of archived plans: 44 items below `verification/corpus/decision/question/`, 15 below `verification/corpus/decision/own-words/`, and 18 scenarios below `verification/question-loop/scenarios/`. Their question items grade a decider on the operator's actual choice. A decider without the plan's whole context cannot be graded on that choice; what it can be graded on is whether the question needed the operator at all. The data stays private: nothing of it enters a public repository.

* *Question items become escalate-or-decide items*: `input.choices` `escalate` and `decide`; the reference `escalate` where the record shows the decision needed the operator (a product behaviour, a scope, an answer against the asker's recommendation, a fact only the operator had), `decide` where the facts in the item settle it; the operator's actual choice kept in `review.notes`, never as the target. An item whose record does not show which applies is set `review.status: contested` with the reason.
* *Unchanged*: the own-words items (they grade the interpretation of words that were given, which needs no plan context) and the scenario files of the question loop.
* *Checked*: every item against the corpus rules of `CorpusValidator` of `pm-mcp-core` (the schema, the canary, the transient-name ban), run over a copy of the corpus with the items placed in it, as the README of `verification/` describes.
