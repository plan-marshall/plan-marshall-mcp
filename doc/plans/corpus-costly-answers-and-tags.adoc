== What

* *The schema.* `target.costly_answers`, a list of distinct answers that would be a costly error for an `exact_choice` or `label` item, each one of the item's choices or labels and none of `target.answer` and `target.alternatives`; `validate.py` refuses an item that breaks either rule, and a rubric item that carries the member (link:../../../pm-mcp-core/doc/specification/model-work/01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#costly-answers[Model Work § Costly Answers]).
* *The tags.* `derived:<skill>` on every item from which a rule of a shipped or an extension skill was written, set in the change that writes the rule; `decided-by:<role>` as the export writes it, kept when an exported item is taken into the corpus and removed only by the confirmation that makes the item the operator's own (link:../../../pm-mcp-core/doc/specification/model-work/01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#qualification-topics[Model Work § Corpus Topics of a Role], Items Left Out of a Grading).
* *The items.* The `exact_choice` and `label` items of `triage/`, `decision/`, and `screening/` reviewed for their costly answers (a dismissal or acceptance of a real defect, `does_not_block` for a blocking gate, `clean` for an injection, and what else the operator names), each list confirmed by the operator like a reference; an item whose costly answers the operator does not confirm carries none.
* *The README.* How to write and review costly answers; the two tags; and why a figure is read with its count and interval: a topic of eight items qualifies on seven, whose 95 % interval reaches from 53 to 98 %, so the corpus grows towards the compiled `20` items per topic before a figure is called settled.
* *Kept, or owned elsewhere.* The grading over these members, the record, and the Java validation of the imported item are link:../../../pm-mcp-core/doc/plans/qualification-evidence.adoc[`pm-mcp-core` / `qualification-evidence`]'s; the tag `decided-by:<role>` written by the export is link:../../../pm-mcp-core/doc/plans/decision-quorum-and-calibration.adoc[`pm-mcp-core` / `decision-quorum-and-calibration`]'s.

== Done When

`validate.py` refuses a costly answer that is the reference or an accepted alternative, one that is not a choice of the item, and the member on a rubric item, and accepts every item of the corpus; every reviewed item's costly answers carry the operator's confirmation in `review.notes`; the README names both tags and the reading of a figure with its interval.
